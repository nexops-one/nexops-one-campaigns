# Demo Bank S.A. (fictitious): exit plan excerpt for core banking (CTR-2023-006)

Provider: Ledgerline Core Systems S.A. (fictitious). Supported functions: customer account management, payment execution, treasury and liquidity management.

## Triggers

Material breach, insolvency of the provider, supervisory instruction, or a failed recovery test with no remediation within 90 days.

## Exit steps

1. Notify the provider and the competent authority.
2. Freeze changes and export the ledger and customer master data in the agreed open formats.
3. Migrate to the pre-assessed alternative platform, running both systems in parallel for one month-end close.
4. Confirm the deletion of data held by the provider and its subcontractors.

## Test

The data export was last tested on 2025-09-10 with a full restore into the alternative environment.

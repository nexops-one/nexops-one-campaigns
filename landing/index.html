Questions

What compliance officers ask us

Who is liable if the CSSF rejects a filing?

The entity files and remains responsible for its register. The Health Check and the compliance engine are readiness and validation aids, not legal advice or certification. Our job is to catch errors before the regulator does.

What exactly does the Health Check check?

We start with the mandatory-field checks from the ESA validation rules. The report lists each finding, where it sits in your register and how to fix it.

Does our register data leave our environment?

For the Health Check you send us a copy under NDA through a secure link, and we delete it once the report is delivered. The compliance engine itself runs in your own environment, so day to day your register stays with you.

Is this SaaS?

No. The compliance engine is self-hosted. It installs in your infrastructure with a Helm chart, and the core is open source under Apache-2.0. That is the point: it doesn't become another ICT third party you have to register and assess.

We use a consultant or a Big 4 firm. Why change?

You don't have to. Many advisors are stretched by smaller clients, and our channel licence lets a ManCo, fund administrator or consultancy run many entities from one install. Validation-first software makes each filing cheaper to prepare and easier to evidence.

What happens after March 2027?

The register needs to stay accurate all year, and the validation rules change every year. The annual subscription keeps the rule catalogs maintained. Once your dependencies are mapped, NEXOPS ONE™ adds cost, concentration and exit analysis for your cloud providers.

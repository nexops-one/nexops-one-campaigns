# Brag plan: compliance-engine by NEXOPS ONE™ (combined promo)

**Source runs combined:** `brag-output/` (the 2:44 compliance-engine product demo: real console captures, "Score 100% / coverage 47%" hook) and `brag-output-1/` (the 21 s Register Health Check promo: CSSF question, ~40% stat, brand identity, CTA).
**Requested:** a professional promotion of compliance-engine that opens and closes on the NEXOPS ONE™ logo. Two stories plus two logo cards don't fit 20 s without rushing the text, so it runs **47 s**, a standard social promo length.

## Answers
- **What is it?** compliance-engine, NEXOPS ONE's open-source engine: it ingests a DORA register, checks it against versioned control catalogues (DORA, GDPR, EU AI Act) and reports readiness with an explicit coverage metric.
- **For whom?** Compliance officers at Luxembourg ManCos, AIFMs and PSFs facing the CSSF register checks in March 2027.
- **What sets it apart?** Missing data is `not_assessed`, never a pass. Evidence is pinned by sha256. Approvals are tied to an exact evaluation. It runs in your own environment and has an open-source core.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Score 100%, with coverage 47%." Also: only ~40% of entities had filed by 16 March (EY, citing supervisory data).
- **Visual hook:** the site's question set large in navy on white, then the 100% / 47% pair.
- **Real UI:** console captures from `compliance-engine demo` (import dry run, controls, evidence verified, approved), the logo lockup, and the real "Book your free Health Check" button.
- **Tone:** `polished`. It's a regulated audience, so long holds, dips through the background, and one accent colour.
- **Caption:** see share-copy.txt.

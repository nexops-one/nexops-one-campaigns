# Demo Bank S.A. (fictitious): ICT third-party risk policy

Version 3.1, approved by the management body of the fictitious Demo Bank S.A. on 2025-11-30.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers every contractual arrangement on the use of ICT services provided by ICT third-party service providers, including intragroup providers and subcontractors that support critical or important functions.

## 2. Register of information

The ICT risk function keeps the register of information complete and current. Every ICT third-party service provider is identified with its legal entity identifier, legal name, person type, headquarters country and ultimate parent undertaking.

## 3. Pre-contractual assessment

Before entering an arrangement, the business owner documents the criticality of the supported function, the substitutability of the service, the locations where data is stored and processed, and the exit strategy.

## 4. Monitoring and review

Arrangements supporting critical or important functions are reviewed at least once a year. The register is reconciled with the procurement system every quarter.

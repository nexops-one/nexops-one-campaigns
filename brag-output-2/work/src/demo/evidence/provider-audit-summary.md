# Demo Bank S.A. (fictitious): ICT provider master data review, Q4 2025

Reviewer: ICT risk function of the fictitious Demo Bank S.A.

## Result

All twelve ICT third-party service providers in the register were checked against their contracts and public registries:

- identification code, code type, legal name, person type and headquarters country are present for every provider;
- the ultimate parent undertaking is recorded where one exists, and is itself registered as a provider;
- one provider record had an invalid country code and was corrected at source.

## Follow-up

Data storage and processing locations are missing for three service lines and three exit plan answers are still open. Owners have been asked to complete them before the next reporting date.

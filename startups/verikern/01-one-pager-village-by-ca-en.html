VerikernSovereign AI gateway for banks and insurers

Luxembourg · October 2026

Proving, request by request, that a bank's AI complies with the AI Act.

The problem

Credit scoring and insurance pricing are high-risk uses under the AI Act. CSSF-supervised banks and insurers must show conformity evidence by 2 December 2027. Today:

  • AI requests go to US-controlled models, with no proof of where the data went;
  • evidence is gathered by hand, through questionnaires, after the fact;
  • auditors and the CSSF ask for a traceable record that no current tool produces for the AI Act.

The solution

  1. Route every request to an approved model, according to a sovereignty level set for each AI system.
  2. Block what is not allowed: strictly confidential data never leaves the bank.
  3. Log every request in a signed, tamper-evident record (hashes, not content).
  4. Generate the AI Act technical file from that log.

Runs on the bank's own Kubernetes or OpenShift, with no outbound traffic to Verikern, or as SaaS in a Luxembourg datacentre.

What sets us apart

  • Compliance as code, not questionnaires.
  • Verifiable without trusting us: the log format and the verifier are published.
  • Sovereign and neutral: the bank keeps its keys (HSM), its data and its choice of models.
  • One record, three obligations: AI Act, DORA's ICT register, GDPR.

Customer and business model

  • Buyer: CIO or head of IT risk; sign-off by the compliance officer.
  • First pilot: an internal GenAI assistant. Fast to deploy, no customer-facing risk, and it builds evidence the bank can reuse for its high-risk models.
  • Pricing: €25k to €50k per AI system per year, evidence pack and label included. Range still to be validated.

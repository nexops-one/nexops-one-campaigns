# Brag Plan: Register Health Check by NEXOPS ONE™ (narrated)

## What is this app?
A free validation report on a Luxembourg entity's DORA register of information. It finds what will fail the CSSF checks before the March 2027 filing, and every finding comes with where it sits and how to fix it, within 48 hours.

## The angle
The reassuring version of a scary question. The site opens with "Will your DORA register pass the CSSF checks in March 2027?" The video asks it, shows why that's a real risk, and then shows the answer as the product actually delivers it: a report that builds row by row (Fails, Fails, Not assessed, Complete). A calm voice (Kokoro `af_bella`, local) carries the story. It adds context that the screen doesn't show, so it never just reads the text aloud.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The site's H1, set large in navy on white and built line by line. Under it the voice says: "A register the CSSF accepted last year can still be rejected next time."

## Key moments
- "~40%" counts up in cyan on navy: the share of entities that had filed by 16 March. The year is left out, as it is on the site; the README asks for it to be confirmed first.
- A file chip, "register-2026.xlsx", drops into an upload panel, and a "Running validation checks" bar fills.
- The Health Check report card from the hero builds itself. First the stat row (14 mandatory fields missing / 6 not assessed / 48h turnaround), then four finding rows, one by one, each with its badge.
- The "Not assessed" badge is stamped next to "Missing data is never counted as a pass."

## Outro / punchline
NEXOPS ONE™ lockup with the real CTA button "Book your free Health Check", which a cursor clicks. Trust line underneath: "Report within 48 hours · Handled under NDA · Free for the first 20 entities".

## User flow worth showing
Send last year's register (file drop) → validation checks run (progress) → report with located findings (rows + badges).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm compliance briefing with a confident narrator
- Interpretation: the narrator sets the pace. Long holds, one accent colour per scene, soft crossfades and dips through navy, and no gimmicks for this regulated audience.

## Audio direction
- Role: narration first, with a warm, low bed underneath
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1` (120 BPM), held at ~0.12 under the voice, rising and fading out over the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset read. The intro is beatless until 3.02 s, which leaves the hook clean. Strong cues at 17.02 s (scene 4 cut) and 20.02 s (CTA cut). Beat grid every 0.50 s; the report rows land every other beat (1.0 s apart) so each one can be read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. The voice carries the energy, and pulsing would fight the regulated tone.
- SFX posture: sparse and matched to motion. A drop on the file chip, soft card-places on the rows, a soft impact on the stamp, and a click on the CTA.
- Restraint rule: no SFX under a spoken word's peak; nothing bright or hissy.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Hook — 0.0–5.2 s
White. The H1 builds line by line, with a sky-to-primary bar growing on the left and the eyebrow "DORA register of information · Luxembourg".
Sequential: three lines, ~0.5 s apart, then the full question holds for 3 s. VO: vo1 at 0.4 s. Transition: dip to navy.

### Scene 2 — Stakes — 5.2–9.0 s
Navy. "~40%" counts up in cyan. "of entities required to file a register had done so as of 16 March", then the source "CSSF · EY, citing supervisory data". VO: vo2 at 5.5 s. SFX: soft impact as the count lands. Transition: crossfade to wash.

### Scene 3 — Flow — 9.0–17.0 s
Wash background. An upload panel: a "register-2026.xlsx" chip drops in, and "Running validation checks" fills to 100%. The panel then gives way to the report card: header "Register Health Check", stats 14 / 6 / 48h, then the rows B_05.01 Fails, B_02.01 Fails, B_06.01 Not assessed, B_01.01 Complete, 1.0 s apart, with the full card held. VO: vo3 at 9.3 s. SFX: drop, then a card-place per row.

### Scene 4 — Principle — 17.0–20.0 s (beat-locked 17.02)
White. "Missing data is never counted as a pass." with a large "Not assessed" badge stamped beside it. VO: vo4 at 17.3 s. SFX: soft impact on the stamp.

### Scene 5 — CTA — 20.0–24.5 s (beat-locked 20.02)
Navy. The folded-N mark facets fold in and the wordmark wipes in. The "Book your free Health Check" button rises, a cursor clicks it, and the trust line appears. VO: vo5 at 20.3 s. Music rises to ~0.3 after the voice ends, then fades out.

**Music mood:** warm, upbeat, understated.
**Audio summary:** the voice leads from the first frame, the bed stays low and beatless under the hook, the beat carries the flow, and the music opens up only once the last line is spoken.

*Privacy:* no real names, emails or client data. The report rows are the site's own illustrative example.

# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Register Health Check (narrated)

## Objective
A short, narrated launch video for the NEXOPS ONE™ Register Health Check.

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output-2026-10-25-020200/composition/`
- Render: `brag-output-2026-10-25-020200/brag.mp4`
- 1920x1080, 30 fps, 24.5 s (length set by the voiceover)

## Source Material

## Creative Direction
Preset `polished`: a calm compliance briefing with a confident narrator. See `brag-plan.md` for the angle, storyboard and voiceover table. Avoid generic SaaS language and abstract filler.

## Visual Identity
Navy `#101841`, primary `#0072b8`, sky `#00abed`, cyan `#00d0fb` (on navy only), paper `#fff`, wash `#f1f5f9`, line `#d6dde7`; status colours from site.css. IBM Plex Sans (`assets/fonts`, @font-face) and IBM Plex Mono (bundled). Logo paths from `brand.svg`, inlined.

## Audio
- Voice: five Kokoro `af_bella` clips (`assets/vo1–5.wav`), each on its own `<audio>`, at the start times listed in the plan.
- Music: `assets/music/bed.mp3` (vol-1, 120 BPM), 0.12 under the voice, raised at the end with a fade-out.
- Beat locks: scene 4 at 17.0 s (cue 17.02), scene 5 at 20.0 s (cue 20.02). The report rows land 1.0 s apart (every other beat).
- SFX: `drop_001` on the file chip, `card-place-1..4` on the rows, `impactSoft_medium_001` on the 40% landing and on the stamp, `mouseclick1` on the CTA click, `bong_001` on the logo.
- Audio-reactive: none; the narration leads.

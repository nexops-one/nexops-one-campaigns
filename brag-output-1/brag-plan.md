# Brag plan: NEXOPS ONE Register Health Check

**What it is:** a free DORA Register Health Check. A Luxembourg fund manager sends last year's register and gets every finding, and how to fix it, within 48 hours, before the CSSF rejects the 2027 filing.
**For:** compliance officers at ManCos, AIFMs and smaller PSFs that don't have a DORA team.
**What sets it apart:** validation-first and self-hosted. "The DORA tool that doesn't create a new DORA problem."
**Most impressive claim:** close to the deadline, only about 40% of entities had filed (EY, citing supervisory data).
**Visual hook:** the site's own question, set big in navy on white, line by line.
**Real UI shown:** the report card from the hero (rendered from the site's markup and CSS), the "Book your free Health Check" button, and the new logo lockup.
**Tone:** `polished`, which suits a regulated, serious audience: long holds, soft dips through the background, one accent colour.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Visual identity
The site's real stylesheet (`landing/assets/site.css`), IBM Plex Sans and Mono, `brand.svg`. Navy `#101841`, primary `#0072B8`, sky `#00ABED`, cyan `#00D0FB` (on navy only). The two-tone bar from the mark is the recurring motif.

## Sound
D major, 96 bpm, soft. A warm pad (D – Bm – G – A, one chord per bar), a gentle plucked arpeggio from scene 3, and a sub-bass note under each chord. Effects are pitched into the key and sent through the same reverb: soft filtered-noise swells on the scene dips, a low muted thump when 40% lands, quiet chord-tone plucks for each finding row, and a soft high pluck on the button click. Mixed with the effects under the music, peaks at −1 dBFS.
